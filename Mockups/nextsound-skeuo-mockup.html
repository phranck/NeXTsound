Demo

Mockup for the NeXTsound Mac app. The front panel is the main window, and the Library panel beside it takes the place of the app's sidebar. The controls follow the skeuomorphickit reference. Sliders have light caps in recessed tracks with a scale on both sides. Band and display color are latching key banks, the on/off settings are slide toggles with a lit legend, and an LED shows what is selected or on. Sliders take the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End. Everything phosphor-colored is live. The big display plots the response of the five-band cascade of RBJ peaking filters, together with the high-pass when it is enabled. Its right side shows the SoundBox's status, its metadata and the latest message. Owner, unit, date and note are the fields planned in issue #39, so they show sample values. A double click or Return renames the selected profile. The demo buttons switch what the SoundBox reports.
